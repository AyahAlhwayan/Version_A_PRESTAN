CPR AR MISSION

Interactive Training

Camera: Allow camera access.

Marker: Place the HIRO marker at the predefined marked position beside the CPR manikin, then point the camera at it.

Training: Read the instruction shown for each step and continue through the same 13-step CPR sequence.

Physical feedback: The PRESTAN LED panel appears at compression step 10. Its states come from the original manikin monitor.

30 compressions: Perform the physical set on the manikin and confirm completion. The original rate-monitor LEDs do not provide a one-to-one compression count.

Physical PRESTAN mode: During Step 10 press START COMPRESSIONS, perform 30 chest compressions on the manikin, then press FINISH PHYSICAL SET. The PRESTAN LEDs provide live monitor status; the system does not count individual compressions.
